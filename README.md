# Global Superstore Sales Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview
This project uses Microsoft Power BI to analyze the Global Superstore dataset and evaluate business performance across sales, profit, products, customers and geographical regions.

The objective is to transform transactional data into actionable insights that support informed business decisions.

## Business Questions
- How do sales and profit change over time?
- Which markets and regions generate the highest sales and profit?
- Which product categories and subcategories perform best?
- Which products or regions experience losses?
- How do discounts relate to profitability?
- Which customer segments contribute most to business performance?

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query for data cleaning and transformation
- DAX for measures and KPIs
- Data visualization and business intelligence

## Analysis Process
1. Dataset exploration
2. Data cleaning and transformation
3. Data modeling
4. KPI development
5. Business performance analysis
6. Dashboard design
7. Insight generation and recommendations

## Key Performance Indicators
The project examines relevant business metrics, including:
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity Sold
- Profit Margin
- Average Discount
- Shipping Cost

*The final KPI list reflects the measures implemented in the dashboard.*

## Dashboard
The Power BI dashboard presents business performance through visualizations and comparisons across relevant time periods, markets, regions, product categories, and customer segments.
