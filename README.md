# Global Superstore Sales Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project analyses Global Superstore sales data using Microsoft Power BI to evaluate business performance, identify profitability drivers and develop actionable recommendations. The analysis focuses on sales, profit, discounting, regional performance, product categories, customer segments and seasonality.

The goal is to demonstrate how business intelligence can turn raw data into insights that support better business decisions.

## Tools Used

- **Microsoft Power BI:** Data analysis, visualization, and dashboard development
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning and transformation, where applied
- **DAX:** KPI and measure calculations, where applied

## Key Performance Indicators

The analysis examines business performance through metrics including:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Total Orders
- Quantity Sold
- Average Discount

## Key Insights

1. **Technology drives profit:** Technology generated $664K in profit at a 14.0% margin, contributing 45% of total profit. Furniture accounted for 32.5% of sales but only 19% of profit. Its Tables sub-category lost $64K at a −8.5% margin.

2. **Sales do not always translate to profit:** Central led with $2.82M in sales and $311K in profit. Southeast Asia ranked fifth in sales but 12th in profit, generating $18K at a 2.0% margin. North Asia ranked sixth in sales but third in profit, with a 19.5% margin.

3. **Discounting affects profitability:** Southeast Asia and EMEA had average discounts of 27% and 20%, respectively, compared with 5% in North Asia and 7% in Central Asia. The lower-discount regions recorded stronger margins.

4. **Profit is spread across products:** The top profit-generating product, Canon imageCLASS 2200 Copier, contributed $25.2K, or 1.7% of total profit. The top 10 products accounted for 8.9% of total profit and seven were Technology products.

5. **Sales are seasonal and Consumer-led:** November and December together accounted for 21.8% of annual sales. February was the lowest-sales month. Consumer generated 51.5% of sales, while Home Office had the highest segment margin at 12.0%.

## Key Risks

- **Heavy discounting:** Lines discounted by 30% or more represented 21% of all lines and generated a combined loss of $813K.
- **Low-margin regions and products:** Southeast Asia, EMEA and the Tables sub-category require profitability reviews.
- **Sales concentration:** More than one-fifth of annual sales occurred in November and December, while the Consumer segment accounted for over half of sales.

## Key Opportunities

- **Review discount policies:** Bringing lines discounted by 30% or more to break-even could potentially improve profit by approximately $813K, assuming other factors remain unchanged.
- **Improve regional profitability:** Investigate pricing practices in higher-margin regions and assess whether they can be adapted to weaker-performing regions.
- **Prioritize profitable growth:** Build on Technology's 14.0% margin and explore opportunities to grow Home Office, which has the highest segment margin but contributes 18% of sales.

## Recommendations

1. **Control discounting:** Require approval for discounts above 30% and establish minimum profit margin thresholds for products.
2. **Review regional pricing:** Reassess pricing and cost-to-serve in Southeast Asia and EMEA.
3. **Improve Furniture profitability:** Review Tables pricing, supplier costs and the viability of loss-making products.
4. **Prioritize Technology:** Protect stock availability for profitable products, explore product bundles and negotiate supplier terms.
5. **Learn from high-performing regions:** Investigate the pricing and product strategies used in North Asia, Central Asia and North.
6. **Plan for seasonality:** Test January–February promotions and prepare inventory and staffing ahead of November–December.
7. **Expand profitable segments:** Explore suitable bulk and business-to-business offers for Corporate and Home Office customers.
8. **Improve dashboard monitoring:** Add profit margin percentage and margin breakdowns by region and category.
